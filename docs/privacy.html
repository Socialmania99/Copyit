Copyit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Copyit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you save stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from your use of Copyit. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

The text, links, code, images, videos, tags, folders and settings you save are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage (chrome.storage.local). They stay on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer or to any third party. Removing the extension, or using "Clear all clips", deletes them.

3. Clipboard access

Copyit reads your clipboard only when you press "Save clipboard" or paste into Copyit. It does not monitor the clipboard in the background. It writes to your clipboard only when you press Copy or use a "Copy as" option.

4. Optional link preview

If "Preview links on hover" is turned on, Copyit loads the page, image or video thumbnail that a link points to. This sends a normal web request to that site (for example YouTube, for a thumbnail). You can turn this off in Settings for zero network requests.

5. Scan to open

QR codes are generated on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

6. Exports and backups

Files you export (JSON, CSV, TXT, Markdown) are saved to your computer by you. Copyit does not send them anywhere.

7. Permissions

  • storage, unlimitedStorage: save your clips locally.
  • clipboardRead, clipboardWrite: save from and copy to your clipboard when you ask.
  • contextMenus: the right-click save options.
  • downloads: save files you export or download.

Copyit has no host permissions. It does not read website content or your browsing history.

8. Support links

The heart icon opens donation pages (such as PayPal or Ko-fi) in a new tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Copyit shares no data with them.

9. Children

Copyit does not collect data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
